Privacy Policy — Vida+

Last updated: May 2, 2026 · Version: 1.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vida+ ("Application", "we", "our"). Vida+ is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data privacy practices and the rights and choices you have regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • Account & Authentication: Email, password (encrypted), username
  • Profile Data: Gender, age, weight (optional — only for hydration calculations)
  • App Content: Tasks, habits, goals, financial notes, shopping list, well-being records (water intake, sleep, emotions)

2.2 Automatically Generated Data

  • Usage Logs: Actions within the app, habit/task completion times
  • Device Permissions: Access to notifications, calendar, storage
  • Technical Information: App version, Android version, anonymized device ID

2.3 Third-Party Data

  • Google Authentication: Verified email (if you sign in with Google)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • ✅ Provide, maintain, and improve Vida+
  • ✅ Sync your data across devices (via Supabase)
  • ✅ Send reminder notifications (habits, tasks, shopping, well-being)
  • ✅ Generate personalized insights and reports
  • ✅ Respond to technical support
  • ✅ Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage

4.1 Local Data

  • Automatically synced with our cloud
  • Remains on the device even when offline
  • Deleted when you uninstall the app

4.2 Cloud Data (Supabase PostgreSQL)

  • Database: PostgreSQL hosted on secure infrastructure
  • Encryption: In transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest
  • RLS (Row-Level Security): Only you can access your data
  • Backups: Daily and stored in multiple regions
  • Retention: Kept while your account is active

4.3 Deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time
  • All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Except data legally required (taxes, compliance)

5. Android Permissions

6. Data Sharing

Your data is NOT shared with:

  • ❌ Advertisers or ad networks
  • ❌ Social networks
  • ❌ Third parties without consent

Your data is shared with:

  • ✅ Supabase (backend) — under processing agreement
  • ✅ Legal authorities — when required

7. Security

  • Authentication with encrypted password (bcrypt)
  • RLS (Row-Level Security) at user level
  • TLS 1.3 for transmission
  • JWT tokens with automatic expiration
  • Regular audits

8. Your Rights (LGPD/GDPR)

  • 📋 Access your data
  • ✏️ Correct information
  • 🗑️ Delete your account
  • 📤 Export data
  • 🚫 Object to processing

Contact: vidaplus.app@gmail.com

9. Retention

  • Account Data: deleted 30 days after account removal
  • Logs: deleted after 90 days
  • Backups: up to 30 days after deletion

10. Changes

We may update this policy. You will be notified of material changes.
